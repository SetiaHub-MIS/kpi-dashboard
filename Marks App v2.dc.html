Checklist Mingguan
as an app

Rebuilt on the real forms: KP staff checklist (22 perkara, 1–5), WS supervisor checklist (17 perkara), and the Checklist Kedai asset log. Same scoring, same roles — SV/AS marks, MANAGER verifies.

What I read from the files
One sheet per person (KP0093…MY0644, WS0001, WS0012), 12 monthly blocks, 4 weeks each.
Every perkara is scored twice a week — once by SV/AS, once by MANAGER — then JUMLAH MARKAH and %.
Most weeks read #DIV/0!. Only Jan, and only the SV column, is really filled. The MGR column is empty all year.
Catatan is where the value is: "2 unit a/c tak sejuk", "sdh report dlm group" — the same issue reappears for months.
Design calls
The dashboard leads with the empty cells. An average built from one filled week is the workbook's core problem, so the grid shows exactly which person × week has no mark.
Kategori collapse, sub-perkara expand. 22 lines won't fit a phone; the marker sees 7 kategori and opens one at a time, with a "semua 4" shortcut per kategori.
MANAGER is a verify pass, not a re-mark. Manager sees the SV score and agrees or adjusts — that's why the second column never got filled by hand.
Catatan carries forward. An open aset issue reappears on next week's form until someone closes it with a tarikh penyelesaian.
Open questions
The KP sheet totals to 95 (19 lines) but the form lists 22 perkara — which three aren't scored?
Should the app be in Bahasa Melayu only, or bilingual? I kept the form labels verbatim.
Is one kedai per manager, or does an Area Manager compare kedai? That changes the top of the dashboard.
Should staff see their own markah, or only supervisors and above?
